Optimization: Models, Algorithms, and Decisions

An optimization model turns a decision into a feasible set and an objective. A solver can search that set, but it cannot decide whether the model captures the decision that matters. This study edition develops both responsibilities: formulating the right problem and understanding what an algorithm’s answer establishes.

The course runs from linear programming and network models through smooth nonlinear methods, geometric programming, dynamic programming and multicriteria choice. Sixteen chapters cover all forty recordings in the supplied NPTEL playlist, about 38.4 hours of teaching. The original course is Optimization, IIT Kharagpur, by Prof. A. Goswami and Dr. Debjani Chakraborty. Official course.

Each chapter supplies definitions, derivations, worked constructions, six exercises with full solutions, and a deterministic Python laboratory. Added examples and labs are teaching constructions; any reused numerical data are attributed. Timestamp links locate related lecture discussions; they do not attribute every added argument to the lecturers.

Evidence boundary. Complete available English caption tracks supply the topic sequence. They are labeled non-automatic by YouTube but can still contain errors. The notes are an independent reconstruction of the mathematics, with separate scientific review and computational checks. They are not an official NPTEL publication. No comprehensive audiovisual audit, human expert certification, or formal proof verification is claimed.

Start with the study guide for prerequisites and the method map. Use the interactive laboratory to explore LP geometry, shadow prices and gradient conditioning. The source map links every recording to its chapter. Mathematical rendering, code and navigation work offline; recordings and external references require internet access.
