# Visual-source coverage boundary

The edition's systematic source pass reads the complete available English caption
tracks for all forty recordings. It does not claim a board/slide transcription,
complete listening, or comprehensive audiovisual fidelity.

Selected board frames were considered for lecture23's bordered-Hessian claim,
lecture24's optimality assumptions, lecture36's greater-than-posynomial
generalization, and lecture40's AHP/TOPSIS formula and ranking ambiguities.
A bounded lecture40 clip request failed because the selected combined video
format was not available. A subsequent format inventory showed separate video
and audio streams, but no source frame was acquired or inspected in this pass.

Consequently, the notes describe these as caption/source ambiguities and supply
independently derived mathematics. They do not assert that a particular board
equation was visually verified or that a caption error proves an instructor error.
The public source manifest records visual and audio review as not performed.

Rendered screenshots of the authored reader are presentation checks only and
must not be confused with frames from the source recordings. Temporary captures,
failed-acquisition files and calibration copies are removed at final cleanup;
compact raw captions remain available locally.
